Repair, resizing and polishing

Repair & Restoration

Repair, resizing, polishing and restoration of fine jewellery, with each item documented at intake and returned in the condition agreed.

What we do

Worn, damaged or inherited pieces are assessed at the bench and returned to you with a written estimate before any work begins. Each item is described and photographed at intake so its condition is on record.

Ring resizing, polishing, repair and full restoration of older pieces are all carried out here. We will tell you when a repair is not worth the cost, or when a piece should be left as it is.

How it works

  1. IntakeYour piece is described, photographed and given a reference. You receive a copy.
  2. Assessment and estimateWe assess the work required and send a written estimate for your authorization.
  3. Work and returnWork proceeds only once you approve. The piece is inspected and returned in the condition agreed.
  • Assessment and written estimate
  • Ring resizing and polishing
  • Documented intake and custody
  • Your authorization before work proceeds

Items in our care

Pieces left with us are held securely and released only to the person named at intake or someone they authorize in writing.
