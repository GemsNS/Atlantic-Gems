Professional setting and resetting

Stone Setting

Professional stone setting for new work and for stones being moved into an existing or new mount.

What we do

Setting is where a stone and its mount meet, and it is done by hand at the bench. We set faceted and cabochon stones into new work and into existing mounts, and we reset stones that have loosened or been removed.

Stones are inspected before setting and again afterwards. If a stone or mount is not suitable for the setting proposed, we say so before proceeding.

How it works

  1. InspectionStone and mount are examined for fit, condition and any risk in setting.
  2. EstimateA written estimate covering the setting work and any adjustment to the mount.
  3. Setting and checkThe stone is set, the piece is cleaned, and both are inspected before return.
  • Setting of faceted and cabochon stones
  • Resetting into existing mounts
  • Bench work carried out by hand
  • Stones inspected before and after setting
