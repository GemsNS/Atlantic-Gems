Custom Jewellery

Custom jewellery designed and manufactured to your brief, from a single stone you already own to a complete new piece or a small production run.

What we do

A commission begins with a conversation about the piece you have in mind, the stones involved and how it will be worn. From there we prepare drawings and a written quote before any metal is cut.

You may bring your own stones, choose from ours, or combine the two. Existing pieces can be remade into something new, and we manufacture small runs for designers and retailers who need pieces made to their specification.

How it works

  1. ConsultationWe discuss the brief, stones, metal and budget, in person or by email.
  2. Design and quoteDrawings and a written quote for your approval. Nothing proceeds until you confirm.
  3. Fabrication and settingThe piece is made and set at the bench, then inspected before collection.
