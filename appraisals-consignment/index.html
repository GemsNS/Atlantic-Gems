Appraisals & Consignment

Written appraisals of jewellery, gemstones and watches, and consignment sale of pieces you wish to part with, under a written agreement.

What we do

An appraisal from us is a written opinion of value for a stated purpose on a stated date. It describes the item, sets out the basis of the valuation, and is signed. It is not an offer to buy and not a promise of any future sale price.

If you would rather sell a piece than keep it, we can offer it for sale on consignment. Nothing is listed until a written agreement records the item, its condition, the reserve price, our commission and the settlement terms.

How it works

  1. Bring the itemBy appointment. The item is described and photographed at intake and you receive a copy.
  2. Appraisal or agreementFor an appraisal, we examine the item and issue the written document. For consignment, we agree the reserve and terms in writing first.
  3. Document or settlementYou receive the signed appraisal, or, once a consigned item sells, settlement within the period stated in the agreement.
  • Written appraisals with the purpose stated
  • Consignment sale under a written agreement
  • Agreed reserve price before any listing
  • Settlement terms set out in writing

How we describe items

Every appraisal and every consigned item follows our Certification & Disclosure Policy. Consignment terms are set out in our trade terms.
